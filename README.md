# Privacy Policy for FixTracker

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

FixTracker ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

### 1. Information We Collect
* **User-Generated Content:** We collect and store the measurements, categories, locations, product URLs, and textual notes that you explicitly input into the App.
* **Device Photos and Camera:** With your explicit permission, the App accesses your device's camera and photo gallery solely to allow you to attach images to your measurement logs. 
* **Data Storage:** All logs, notes, and pictures are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal project data, photos, or measurements to external cloud servers.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected strictly to provide the core functionality of the App: allowing you to record, organize, and reference your repair project details. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

### 3. Permissions
To function correctly, the App requires access to:
* **Camera:** To take real-time photos of your repairs.
* **Storage/Media:** To save and view images associated with your logs.
You can revoke these permissions at any time via your device settings, though some features may cease to function.

### 4. Security
We implement standard mobile security measures to protect your data locally. However, the security of data stored on your device depends entirely on your device's security settings (passwords, encryption, biometrics).

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on our store listing page.

### 6. Contact Us
